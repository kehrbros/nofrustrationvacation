# Domain: travel incidents

- **Goal:** users store travel routes and are informed about events that affect the course of their trip (e.g. road closure).
- **Architecture** (modules, dependencies, persistence, legacy code): see [architecture.md](architecture.md).
- **Schema:** `V20__trips_and_incidents.sql`, cover photo `V21__trip_cover_image.sql`, travel period and offsets `V22__trip_period_and_offsets.sql`, road network `V23__road_network.sql`, route planning `V24__route_planning.sql`, planning status `V25__route_plan_status.sql`, accommodation bookings `V26__booking_accommodation.sql`, point of the accommodation for spatial queries `V28__accommodation_location.sql`, route suggestions and `waypoint.origin` `V29__recommendations.sql`, booking of alternative accommodations `V30__booking_reservation.sql`, travellers `V31`, traffic feed `V32`–`V37` (see [traffic-feed.md](traffic-feed.md), [consequence-assessment.md](consequence-assessment.md)), route suggestions no longer stored `V38__drop_suggestion_sets.sql`, enrichment `V39`–`V41`, `impact` no longer stored `V42`, completion and cancellation of reservations `V43__reservation_recovery_refund.sql`, proposals of the agent `V44__advisor.sql`, places `V45__place.sql`, accommodation search of the agent `V46__advisor_stay.sql`, choosing proposals and to-dos `V47__proposal_decision_booking_todo.sql`, thumbnail of the cover photo `V48__trip_cover_thumbnail.sql`, resolved notices `V49__consequence_resolved.sql`, plans for incidents and their combined payment `V50__incident_plans.sql` (see [incident-plans.md](incident-plans.md)), LiteAPI offer ids untruncated as `TEXT` `V51__long_offer_ids.sql`, guest of the trip `V52__trip_guest.sql`, paid reservations without stop and `booking_todo.reservation_id` `V53__keep_paid_reservation_without_stop.sql`. Next free migration: `V54`.

- **References between modules only by id** (`appUserId`, `segmentId`, `stopId`, `routeId`, …), with a foreign key in the database. JPA relationships (`@ManyToOne`) only within a module.
- **Historised (Envers):** `Trip`, `Stop`, `Waypoint`, `Booking`, `AccommodationDetails`, `Incident` (incl. segments, `incident_segment_aud`). Not historised: `TripCoverImage` (binary data, a replaced image is dropped), road network, `Route`/`RouteSection` (planned, gets replaced), `Consequence` and proposals of the agent (derived), input (immutable except for the processing status).

## Trips

- `Trip` (belongs to one user) 1–* `Stop` (ordered via `position`, coordinates, optional arrival, departure).
- **Only within one region of the road network** (initially New Zealand, `Regions.covers`, see [network.md](network.md#interfaces-networklogic)): stops need coordinates when created and changed, stops and waypoints outside are rejected by `TripLogic` (400 `LOCATION_OUTSIDE_REGIONS`, without coordinates 400 `INVALID_TRIP_INPUT`). Applies to REST, MCP (`TripTools`) and to moving the stop to the booked accommodation (then the stop stays, and so does the booking). The frontend only suggests places in New Zealand.
  - The columns stay nullable: older stops without coordinates remain, on the next change a place must be chosen.
- `Trip` has a travel period `startAt`–`endAt` (points in time as `OffsetDateTime`, both inclusive, required on creation, `endAt` ≥ `startAt`, also as a check constraint). Stored instead of calculated from the stops, so that a trip has a period even without stops. `reschedule(…)` changes it.
- The frontend sends the points in time with the offset of the system time zone (trip from 12 to 26 July: `2027-07-12T00:00:00+02:00` to `2027-07-26T23:59:59+02:00`). The offset is kept, see times in [architecture.md](architecture.md#persistence).
- `Trip.travelers`: number of travellers (optional, 1–99, check constraint, `V31`), suggested as the number of guests when creating a booking (without a value: guests of the last booking of the trip). `changeTravelers(…)` changes it.
- `Trip.guest` (`TripGuest`, embeddable, `V52`): first name, last name, email, phone of the person for whom the trip's hotels are booked. Only set if the user chooses "Remember for this trip" in the booking form, then prefilled for every booking of the trip. Personal data, therefore `@NotAudited` (not in `trip_aud`), removed when forgotten (`DELETE /trips/{id}/guest`) or when the trip is deleted. `PUT /trips/{id}/guest` checks like the reservation (required fields, email, phone), error `INVALID_TRIP_INPUT` with `field`.
- `Trip.phaseAt(pointInTime)` returns `UPCOMING`, `CURRENT` or `PAST` (comparison of the points in time, offset irrelevant) and controls the trip list: current trip at the top, below tabs "Upcoming" (incl. further current trips) and "Past", each ascending by `startAt`.
- Arrival and departure of a `Stop` also keep their offset.
- Existing trips without a period received `createdAt` (UTC) as start and end during the migration.
- **Changing stops** (`TripLogic`): insert (at a position or at the end), change, move, delete. Afterwards the stops are renumbered and the routes reconciled: routes between neighbours that remain neighbours stay (with waypoints and sections), missing ones are created, the rest are deleted with their waypoints (waypoints do not move to the new route).
- A trip is deleted with routes, waypoints and stops, the database deletes cover photo and consequences along with it.
- Before deleting stops (individually or with the trip) `TripLogic` publishes `StopsDeleting` in the transaction. The module bookings then deletes the bookings and booking attempts (`BookingReservation`) at these stops. When deleting single stops: if a payment is currently running at one of the stops (`AUTHORIZED`, `PROVIDER_BOOKED`, `NEEDS_ATTENTION`), it rejects with `StopInUseException` (409 `STOP_IN_USE`), so that no reserved money remains without a booking attempt. When deleting the whole trip it never rejects: all reservations of the trip are deleted (also running ones and paid ones of stops deleted earlier, found via the trip's to-dos), nothing is cancelled or refunded. The user confirms a warning first (Philipp, 2026-11-12: cleaning up for the hackathon submission, not a production behaviour).
- `Trip` 1–0..1 `TripCoverImage` (cover photo, `tripId` unique): origin `GENERATED` (created in the backend, e.g. via image model) or `UPLOADED` (by the user), `contentType`, image data (`content`, `BYTEA`, lazily loaded), `createdAt`, thumbnail for trip lists (`thumbnailContentType`, `thumbnailContent`, empty for images that cannot be decoded, e.g. WebP; recalculated with `replace(…)`). Separate table so that trip lists do not load the image data; deleted with the trip (`ON DELETE CASCADE`). `replace(…)` swaps the image, e.g. an upload instead of a generated image.
- `Route`: one instance per trip, connects exactly 2 stops (`fromStop`, `toStop`, unique per pair). If another trip drives the same way, it has its own route.
- `Route` 1–1..* `RouteSection`, ordered (`position` from 0, unique per route). Every section points to exactly one segment (`segmentId`) and has the expected passage as a period `expectedPassageStart`–`expectedPassageEnd`.
- `RouteLogic.replaceSections(routeId, sections)` replaces all sections of a route and publishes `RouteChanged`.
- **`Waypoint`:** point via which the user wants to drive between two stops, belongs to a route, ordered (`position`, unique per route), without a stay. Coordinate required, optional name and road hint (e.g. `SH 73`). The coordinate (intention) is stored, the snapped segment is determined anew on every planning. Historised, the reference to the route is not (the route is not historised).
  - `origin`: `USER` (by the user) or `SUGGESTION` (created when choosing a route suggestion), set on creation and unchanged afterwards, even if the user changes the waypoint. REST and MCP always create `USER`.
- The waypoints of a route are replaced as an ordered list: with id changed, without id created, missing ones deleted.
- **Planning:** `RoutePlanning.plan(routeId)` fetches stops and waypoints, calls `network.Routing` (without incidents, detours are recommendations), calculates the passage from the departure at the start stop plus the durations of the segments before and passes `PlannedSection`s to `RouteLogic.replaceSections`. Routing runs outside the transaction.
  - Result `PlanResult` with `PlanOutcome`: `PLANNED`, `MISSING_COORDINATES` (a stop without coordinates), `MISSING_DEPARTURE` (start stop without departure, no time guessed), `NO_ROUTE` (with reason), `ROUTING_UNAVAILABLE` (road network not loaded yet, plan again later). Except for `PLANNED` the sections stay unchanged.
  - The result is stored on the route (`planOutcome`, `planReason`), so that the user also sees it later (e.g. "departure missing").
  - The arrival at the target stop stays what the user entered. Comparison with the planned arrival is open.
- **When planning happens** (`TripEditing`, for REST and MCP): first the change is committed, then the affected routes are planned. The change remains even if the planning fails.

  | Change | planned routes |
  |---|---|
  | Insert, move, delete a stop | newly created routes |
  | Coordinates of a stop | routes to and from the stop |
  | Departure of a stop (different point in time, not just offset) | route from the stop |
  | Arrival or name of a stop, name or period of the trip | none |
  | Waypoints of a route | this route |
  | Plan again (e.g. after `ROUTING_UNAVAILABLE`) | this route |

- **Discarding sections:** if stops or waypoints of a route change, `RouteLogic.discardSections` removes its sections (with the consequences) immediately in the transaction of the change, `plannedAt` and planning status become empty. If the planning fails afterwards, no incidents match the old path any more. The rule "except for `PLANNED` the sections stay unchanged" thus only applies to planning again without a change.
- **Fractions:** `RouteSection` and `PlannedSection` have `fromFraction`/`toFraction`, which part of the segment is driven (only at the first and last section not 0–1). Prevents false alarms on long segments as soon as `incident_segment` also has fractions.

## Road network

- A `RoadSegment` is one drivable direction of a road from junction to junction with a stable id, generated from OpenStreetMap. Details on key, import and update in [network.md](network.md).
## Bookings

- `Stop` 1–* `Booking` (`stopId`): type (`ACCOMMODATION`, `TRANSPORT`, `ACTIVITY`, `OTHER`), title, provider, booking number, period.
- Only accommodations (`ACCOMMODATION`) are implemented so far. Concept with all input methods: https://claude.ai/artifact/Nc2uQiZfZdib9xT8SN8nTr
- `Booking` additionally:
  - `status`: `CONFIRMED` (default), `CANCELLED` (`cancel()`, no API yet).
  - `source`: how the booking got into the app, `MANUAL`, `DOCUMENT` (confirmation pasted or uploaded), `SHARE` (shared to the app, possibly supplemented with pasted text or a file), `SEARCH` (searched and booked in the app, so far alternative accommodations via LiteAPI; later also voice/name search) and later `EMAIL` (forwarding).
  - `manageUrl` (link to the booking at the provider, http(s) only), `price` + `currency` (ISO 4217), `cancellationDeadline` (free cancellation until), `notes`.
  - `change(…)` replaces everything the user can edit; `source` and `status` stay.
- **Time zone of the accommodation:** check-in (`startsAt`), check-out (`endsAt`) and cancellation deadline are in the local time of the accommodation, not that of the device. The frontend sends them without offset (`LocalDateTime`), the backend determines the zone from the coordinates (timeshape, OSM time zone boundaries, offline) and stores `OffsetDateTime` with the offset valid there (daylight saving time). Without coordinates the zone the frontend sends along applies (`fallbackTimeZone`, zone of the device), otherwise UTC. Offsets in `_tz` columns as for stops. The time is displayed as it applies at the place.
- `Booking` 1–0..1 `AccommodationDetails` (table `booking_accommodation`, `booking_id` unique, `@OneToOne` within the module, deleted with the booking): name, address, country (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2), coordinates (only together, the PostGIS column `location` is derived from them), `timeZone` (IANA, required), phone, email, website, `placeRef` (origin of the place, e.g. `osm:node/123`), house rules `checkInFrom`/`checkInUntil`/`checkOutUntil` (`LocalTime`), guests, rooms (1–99). Separate table so that transport and activities get their own details later and `booking` stays the common core.
- **Not stored:** the PIN of the booking (access secret, not needed) and the original document of the confirmation (privacy).
- **Price:** informative, in the currency in which it was booked (`NUMERIC(12, 2)` + currency), the app does not calculate with it. Deliberate exception from [money.md](money.md) (base currency, `Money`).
- **Ownership:** via the stop (`TripLogic.stop(appUserId, stopId)`), foreign or unknown stops and bookings are 404.
- **Looking up an accommodation** (`AccommodationLookup`, nothing is stored): suggestions for the name from LiteAPI hotel data (`GET /data/hotels`, name contains the input, within 30 km of the stop first, nearest first). For the chosen accommodation address, phone, email, website and house rules (`checkInFrom`/`checkInUntil`/`checkOutUntil`) from `GET /data/hotel`; what is missing there (or without a LiteAPI id, e.g. a place from the map) the assistant searches via OpenAI web search. It only gets name, address, country and coordinates of the accommodation, returns `null` instead of guessing and names sources; values in the wrong format are discarded. The response states per field where it comes from (`fromLiteApi`, `fromAssistant`), researched fields must be checked by the user. The place is stored as `placeRef` `liteapi:<hotelId>`.
- **Period of a booking:** in domain terms only the arrival and departure date count (Philipp, 2026-11-03), form and details show no time. By convention it is stored as `OffsetDateTime` (`startsAt`/`endsAt`) with an internal time: `checkInFrom`/`checkOutUntil`, otherwise the read or stored time, otherwise 15:00/10:00 (local time of the accommodation). When check-in is possible is shown by the house rules.
- **Extracting a confirmation** (`AccommodationExtractor`): pasted text and/or screenshot/PDF (up to 5 MB) go to OpenAI via the module `assistant` and come back as an unsaved draft (`AccommodationDraft`, fields plus `uncertainFields`). The instruction forbids PIN, payment and guest data. Values in the wrong format are discarded and marked as uncertain, links only with `https://`. Only the user's confirmation saves.
- **New stop from a booking:** in the form "Add stop" an accommodation entered there fills place, arrival (check-in) and departure (check-out), where still empty, and is saved after the stop. The times of the stop still follow the system time zone.

- **Stops and bookings:** bookings (except cancelled ones) lie within the days of their stop: check-in not before the day of arrival, check-out not after the day of departure (Philipp, 2026-11-10). Only the days count, the time of a booking is internal. Days are local, the booking in the zone of the accommodation, the stop with its own offset. Without arrival or departure that side is open. No new schema.
  - **Changing a stop** (REST `PUT /trips/{tripId}/stops/{stopId}`, MCP `update_stop`): on a new arrival or departure `TripLogic` publishes `StopRescheduling` in the transaction, `bookings` (`StopReschedulingListener`) checks the bookings at the stop. If they do not fit: 409 `STOP_BOOKINGS_CONFLICT` with the bookings (`trimmable` per booking) and `extendedArrivalAt`/`extendedDepartureAt` (arrival to the earliest check-in day or departure to the latest check-out day, same time, empty if that side fits). With `?bookingConflict=TRIM_BOOKINGS` the bookings are instead shortened to the new days (same time, offset of the accommodation).
  - **Changing a booking** (`PUT /accommodation-bookings/{id}`): outside the days of the stop 409 `BOOKING_OUTSIDE_STOP` with arrival and departure of the stop, both extended to the booking, and `trimmable`. `?stopConflict=EXTEND_STOP` extends the stop in the same transaction (`TripLogic.rescheduleStop`, name and place stay, afterwards the route from the stop is replanned), `TRIM_BOOKING` shortens the booking to the days of the stop.
  - **Not shortenable:** accommodations booked in the app via LiteAPI (`source` `SEARCH`, the booking at the hotel cannot be changed) and bookings of which no night would remain. Then cancelling or extending the stop remain. Shortening only changes the booking in the app, the provider is not notified (note in the dialog).
  - **Not checked:** changes made by the app itself (`BookingFit.IGNORE`): chosen departure (`RouteChoice`), later arrival due to an overnight stay on the way (`ProposalDecisions`, for this there is the to-do `NOTIFY_LATE_ARRIVAL`), moving the stop to the replacement accommodation. Likewise creating a booking (only changes are checked).
  - Frontend: "Edit stop" (pencil in the stop list, `StopSheet` under `stops/:stopId/bearbeiten`) and "Edit booking" show `StayConflictDialog` on 409 with "Extend stop to the booking dates", "Shorten booking(s)" (disabled if not shortenable) and "Cancel".
- **Alternative accommodations** (concept and flow: [accommodation-alternatives.md](accommodation-alternatives.md)): `BookingReservation` (table `booking_reservation`, not historised) is a booking attempt via LiteAPI at the stop (`stopId`), optionally with the replaced booking (`replacesBookingId`) and the resulting one (`bookingId`, unique). Status `PREBOOKED` → `PAYMENT_PENDING` → `AUTHORIZED` → `PROVIDER_BOOKED` → `BOOKED`, or `FAILED` (nothing booked, no money moved). Holds offer, net price, commission, final price, hotel (`ReservationHotel`) and until booking the guest data (deleted afterwards). The resulting `Booking` has `source` `SEARCH`, `placeRef` `liteapi:hotel/<id>`, provider `LiteAPI`.
  - **Stop deleted (`V53`, 2026-11-12):** a reservation that still holds money (`BOOKED`, or `CANCELLED` with the refund due, `holdsMoney()`) is not deleted with its stop: `stopId` becomes `null` (`detachFromStop`, FK `ON DELETE SET NULL`), so it can still be cancelled with a refund from the to-do list and the background job finishes a due refund. All other reservations are deleted with the stop as before. A reservation without stop is no longer reachable via `/booking-reservations/{id}` (404), only via its to-do.
- **Mark as cancelled:** `BookingLogic.cancel` sets `status` `CANCELLED`, only in the app, the provider is not notified.
- **To-do list** (`BookingTodo`, table `booking_todo`, `V47`, not historised, logic `BookingTodos`): bookings the user has to take care of themselves. Never cancel automatically, it is about money (Philipp, 2026-11-07).
  - Per trip (`tripId`, `ON DELETE CASCADE`), `bookingId` optional (`ON DELETE SET NULL`). Copies title, provider, booking number, management link, phone, email, period, cancellation deadline, so that the entry stays usable even after the booking is deleted.
  - `kind`: `CANCEL` (cancel) or `NOTIFY_LATE_ARRIVAL` (inform the accommodation about a later arrival, with `newArrivalOn`). `reason`: `REPLACED` (replacement booked), `STOP_REMOVED` (stop with future bookings deleted, not when deleting the whole trip), `BOOKING_REMOVED` (a single booking made and paid in the app deleted without cancelling it; other bookings deleted by hand need no entry), `ARRIVAL_LATER` (overnight stay on the way pushes back the arrival).
  - Only for confirmed bookings (`CONFIRMED`) that are not over, and only once per booking and kind while open. `bookedInApp`: booked via a reservation of the app (`BOOKED`); `reservationId` (`V53`, FK `ON DELETE SET NULL`) points to it and stays when the booking or its stop is deleted. `cancellableInApp` (open `CANCEL` with `reservationId`): the frontend offers "Cancel" in the app, `POST /booking-todos/{todoId}/refund-cancellation` cancels at LiteAPI, refunds with PayPal and closes the entry (409 `CANCELLATION_NOT_POSSIBLE` otherwise). A cancellation of the reservation closes its open `CANCEL` entries, also those without booking.
  - **Removing a booking made in the app** (2026-11-12, Philipp): deleting such a booking or its stop asks first (`RemoveAppBookingDialog`): "Cancel and remove" (cancels the bookings still free to cancel with refund, then removes), "Only remove" (the booking stays at the hotel and goes on the to-do list, from where it can still be cancelled), "Back". Deleting the whole trip only warns in its confirmation that bookings made in the app are not cancelled, then deletes everything.
  - "Done" (`done_at`) for `CANCEL` also marks the booking as cancelled, "Reopen" only reverts the flag. The frontend therefore sends "Done" only after a 5 s undo hint ([architecture.md](architecture.md#frontend)). If the booking is cancelled in another way, open `CANCEL` entries close.
  - API: `GET /trips/{tripId}/booking-todos`, `POST`/`DELETE /booking-todos/{todoId}/completion`, `POST /booking-todos/{todoId}/refund-cancellation`, foreign or unknown entries 404 `TODO_NOT_FOUND`.

## Input

- `EventSource` (unique name, type `TRAFFIC_FEED` or `WEBHOOK`, active yes/no, last successful fetch, `etag`/`lastModified`) 1–* `Event`.
- `Event` (table `input_event`, one table for all subtypes, column `kind`): raw data `payload`, `externalId` (unique per source, detects duplicate delivery), `subjectId` (id of the event at the source, the same for all versions), `messageHash`, `receivedAt`, `processedAt`, `processingNote`, `enrichmentPending`.
  - `TrafficEvent` (`TRAFFIC`): road, description, period read from the payload.
  - `WebhookEvent` (`WEBHOOK`): signature checked yes/no.
- `EventSource` 1–* `SourceSubject` (unique per source and `subjectId`): last known version of an event of a fetched source, missed fetches, lifted at. State of the reconciliation (`FeedDiff`), see [traffic-feed.md](traffic-feed.md#diffing-feeddiff).
- An event triggers 0..1 incident (`incidentId`). Several events (e.g. updates of a closure) can lead to the same incident: versions of the same `subjectId` change the incident of the previous version. Without an incident the reason is in `processingNote`. Processing by `TrafficEventProcessor`, see [traffic-feed.md](traffic-feed.md#processing-trafficeventprocessor).

## Incidents

- `Incident`: type (`CLOSURE`, `CONGESTION`, `ROADWORKS`, `ACCIDENT`, `OTHER`), description, valid `validFrom`–`validTo` (`validTo` empty = until further notice), expected delay, assumed delay (`assumedDelay`). Located on 1..* segments (`incident_segment`).
- **Type change and assumption (implemented, `V33`):** `IncidentLogic.change` also takes the type (same id, Envers keeps the old type, consequences are updated and keep their id); the previous variant without type stays and keeps type and assumption. `Incident.assumedDelay`: delay the source assumes without a number (e.g. NZTA `Delays` 15 min), historised, counts as estimated. The consequence still only takes over `expectedDelay`; `assumedDelay` is only used by the assessment ([consequence-assessment.md](consequence-assessment.md)).
- **Reported incidents** (`IncidentReporting.report`, from `input`): located via `network.SegmentMatching`, creates or changes the incident of the previous version; "ended" sets `validTo` = now (no deletion). Result incident id or reason (`NO_SEGMENTS`, `AREA_NOT_SUPPORTED`, `ROUTING_UNAVAILABLE`, `ENDED`), see [traffic-feed.md](traffic-feed.md#processing-trafficeventprocessor).
- **Time windows (implemented, `V34`):** `Incident` 0..* `IncidentWindow` (`startsAt`, `endsAt`, `incident_window`, historised in `incident_window_aud`), e.g. a nightly closure over weeks. No windows = continuous. `validFrom`–`validTo` stays the hull for queries; a passage is only affected if it lies in the hull **and** in at least one window (`IncidentOverlap`, also for route suggestions). The consequence remembers the first window hit (`windowStart`, `windowEnd`, empty without windows). Origin and reasoning in [traffic-feed.md](traffic-feed.md#time-windows).
- `Incident` 1–* `Consequence`. Every consequence affects exactly one route (at most one per incident and route) and references the first affected `RouteSection`. Plus `delay` (= expected delay of the incident). `impact` (`CLOSURE` → `BLOCKED`, otherwise `DELAYED`) follows from the type of the incident and is not stored (`V42`).
- **Assessment (implemented, `V35`):** every consequence gets a category `TOO_LATE`, `DELAYED` or `NO_IMPACT`, assessed per route against the arrival of the target stop (`Stop.arrivalAt` = deadline), with accumulated delay. Plus `match` (`DIRECT`, `BY_SHIFT`, `MARGINAL`), `assessedDelay` (empty = not passable), `delayAssumed`, `passageAt` (shifted passage), `expectedArrival`, `deadline`, `assessedAt`. Derived, not historised. `delay` remains the value of the incident. Details in [consequence-assessment.md](consequence-assessment.md).
- `Incident.vehicleScope` (`ALL`, `HEAVY_ONLY`, `LIGHT_ONLY`, from the enrichment, historised): `HEAVY_ONLY` has no effect on cars (`NO_IMPACT`).
- `Incident.timesUnconfirmed` (`V36`, historised): closure with times only in the text, not read yet; type `OTHER`, assessment `DELAYED` without delay, note "check times", see [traffic-feed.md](traffic-feed.md#processing-trafficeventprocessor).
- Consequences are derived and never edited by users; the user can only remove a resolved one from the map (see below). If route or section are deleted, the database deletes the consequence along with them (`ON DELETE CASCADE`).
- **Resolved notices (implemented 2026-11-08, `V49`):** if an incident ends, its consequence is not deleted but marked as resolved (`Consequence.resolvedAt`):
  - "Ended" = `Incident.hasEnded`: `validTo` reached, or called off before the start (`validTo` = `validFrom`, this is how `IncidentReporting` ends a report before its start).
  - Immediately during the assessment (`ConsequenceAssessment`, e.g. the source reports the end), otherwise by the job `ResolvedIncidents` (`ResolvedIncidentsScheduler`, every `nfv.incidents.resolved-check.interval` = 5 min, off in tests) when `validTo` expires without a report. Until the run a consequence with an ended incident already counts as resolved (`isResolved`).
  - Resolved ones no longer count: not in `TripIncidents.active`, counters per trip, summary per route, `ActiveIncidents.consequencesOnRoute` (suggestions, agent) and `IncidentOverview.effects`.
  - `GET /trips/{id}/incidents` returns them after the current ones, most recently resolved first, with `resolved` = true and `resolvedAt`; without places the route has already passed.
  - If the incident applies again (e.g. reported again), `resolvedAt` becomes empty on the next assessment.
  - Removing: `DELETE /trips/{tripId}/routes/{routeId}/incidents/{incidentId}` deletes the resolved consequence (`204`; not resolved `409` `INCIDENT_NOT_RESOLVED`; unknown or foreign `404`). It does not come back, because ended incidents are no longer assessed; if the incident applies again, a new one is created.
  - Frontend: on the map a small icon with a green tick instead of chip and route, tapping opens the notice (`IncidentSheet`) highlighted in green with "Remove notice from the map". Removing is optimistic: the dialog closes, the icon disappears immediately (`TripPage.removeResolvedIncident`), the call runs in the background; if it fails, the icon reappears with a short message next to the map buttons. Notice chips of current incidents are now only the icon in a circle (title as tooltip and `aria-label`). The icon depends on the kind of incident (`trips/incidentIcon.ts`): warning triangle for a notice, three overlapping cars one behind the other seen from the front for a traffic jam, car with impact for an accident, barrier for roadworks, round no-entry sign for a closure. The same icons appear in the stop list on the left in the impact chip of the route row, whose subline only shows distance and driving time.

## Matching logic (`ConsequenceMatcher`)

1. **Incident new or changed** (`IncidentLogic.create`/`change`): segments of the incident → via the index segment → RouteSection (`ix_route_section_segment`) the routes with sections on them whose passage overlaps the extended hull (`validFrom − max-wait − tolerance-max` to `validTo + tolerance-max`), plus the routes with an existing consequence of the incident → every route is assessed as a whole.
2. **Route new or changed** (`RouteChanged`) or **arrival of the target stop changed** (`StopArrivalChanged`), synchronously in the same transaction: the route is assessed as a whole.
- **Assessment** (`ConsequenceAssessment.assessRoute`, rules as a pure function `RouteAssessor`): sections in order, active incidents on their segments (`validTo` empty or not passed), delays add up and shift later passages. Result one consequence per incident hit. Details in [consequence-assessment.md](consequence-assessment.md).
- Overlap: `passageEnd >= validFrom` and (`validTo` empty or `passageStart <= validTo`), with time windows additionally at least one window; the queries stay on the hull. As a pure function `IncidentOverlap` in `incidents.logic`, also for the assessment of route suggestions. Other modules read incidents via `ActiveIncidents.onSegments` (records, no entities).
- Existing consequences are updated (the id stays, for later notifications), those no longer applicable are deleted, unless the incident has ended: then resolved (see above).
- **Tolerance (implemented):** for the check the (shifted) passage is extended by `max(15 min, 10 % of the time since departure)` (at most 2 h). Hits only through the tolerance are "tight" (`MARGINAL`), at most `DELAYED`, see [consequence-assessment.md](consequence-assessment.md#passage-tolerance-decided-2026-11-04).
- Tests: `ConsequenceMatchingTest`, `RouteAssessorTest`.

## Route suggestions

- For a route up to `n` suggestions for how to drive, during planning and when an incident hits the route. Both from a `Scenario` (points, departure, blocks/delays, reference route) with the same logic, details in [recommendations.md](recommendations.md).
- **Proposals of the agent:** at the user's request, automatically for `TOO_LATE` (implemented, switched off for now), an agent proposes solutions. `advisor` stores these runs and proposals (decision 2026-11-06), the user chooses themselves, see [advisor.md](advisor.md).
- Suggestions of `recommendations` are recalculated on every request and not stored. The chosen route comes back as a list of points (`PUT /routes/{id}/choice`), is stored as `Waypoint`s (new ones with `origin` `SUGGESTION`, existing ones of the user stay) and the route is replanned, no segment list. A stored route counts as chosen, there is no separate flag.
- No check for parallel changes, the last choice wins. Later in general via versioning of the entities (`@Version`), for routes including the stops.
- The planned course of a route comes from `RouteLines` (`trips.logic`): line from the sections via `network.SegmentQueries.line`, distance, duration and "via …" from the road hints of the `SUGGESTION` waypoints.

## Open

- **Incidents at accommodations** (e.g. "accommodation not reachable"): deliberately left out, to be considered later. Then an incident may affect a stop or a booking instead of segments.
- **Event → incident:** implemented without enrichment and time windows (stage 2), the rest designed in [traffic-feed.md](traffic-feed.md#stages).
- **Replanning:** after `NetworkRefreshed` (replan affected routes) and automatically planning routes with `ROUTING_UNAVAILABLE` again are still missing. Detour for `BLOCKED` via `recommendations`, draft in [recommendations.md](recommendations.md).
- **Propagating delay:** a delay on a section shifts the expected passage of all following sections; currently not taken into account, planned for the assessment ([consequence-assessment.md](consequence-assessment.md#delay-per-consequence)).
- **End of an incident:** solved by resolved notices (see above). Open: remove resolved notices automatically after some time.
- **More precise impact and delay:** solved by the assessment per route ([consequence-assessment.md](consequence-assessment.md)); calibrating tolerance and assumptions on real data remains open.
- **Bookings:** relation to incidents (e.g. check-in missed due to delay, accommodation not reachable), transport and activities, voice/name search with AI (stage 3, the search for alternatives is a first part of it), email forwarding (stage 4), cancelling via API, times of a stop created from the booking in the zone of the accommodation.
- **Notification:** who is informed when about what, thresholds (e.g. only from 15 min delay).
- **Sharing trips:** currently a trip belongs to exactly one user.
- **Deleting stops with bookings:** `fk_booking_stop` has no `ON DELETE CASCADE`, deleting a stop or trip with bookings fails (bookings and deleting stops/trips are both possible via API). To be decided: delete bookings along with it or reject the deletion.
- **Period and stops:** whether arrival/departure of the stops must lie within the travel period is not checked yet.
- **Creating a booking outside the stop:** not checked so far (e.g. sharing to the app without a dialog), see [Stops and bookings](#bookings).
